title: "Kit: staying safe online, for parents and teachers"
description: "A 60 to 75 minute session for parents and teachers on scams, accounts, privacy, and kids online."
tags: [teach, kit]
---

**Audience:** parents, teachers, school staff. **Group size:** 5 to 40. **Length:** 60 to 75 minutes. **You'll need:** a projector, a phone for live demos, and printed [home checklists](../printables/home-spark-checklist.md).

## Objectives

Participants can:

1. Spot the four pressures behind almost every scam, and verify before acting.
2. Set up unique passwords and two-step login.
3. Name two ways their family's information leaks online, and one way to shrink it.
4. Say what to do, and not do, if a child is threatened or blackmailed online.

## Hook (5 min)

Ask who has gotten a text about a package they didn't order. It's the same trick every time.

## Explanation (20 min)

1. **How scams work** (7 min): urgency, secrecy, authority or emotion, and strange payment ([Learn the scam reflex, pause and verify](../practices/scams-and-social-engineering/scam-pause-and-verify.md)). Have the room spot the pressures in three real examples.
2. **Why accounts get taken over** (7 min): breaches, credential stuffing, and email as the master key. Then password managers and two-step login ([Let a password manager remember for you](../practices/accounts-and-identity/acct-password-manager.md), [Turn on two-step login, starting with your email](../practices/accounts-and-identity/acct-two-step-login.md)).
3. **Kids online** (6 min): the biggest risks are people, not hackers: sextortion, scams in games, and oversharing ([Have the family tech talk, and keep having it](../practices/people-and-culture/ppl-family-tech-talk.md)).

## Activities (30 min)

- **Breach check, live** (10 min): with a volunteer's permission, check their email on Have I Been Pwned on screen ([Find out what's already leaked about you](../practices/accounts-and-identity/acct-breach-check.md)). Then everyone searches their own name and town on their phone ([Get yourself off data broker sites](../practices/privacy-and-footprint/priv-data-broker-opt-out.md)).
- **Two-step login, live** (10 min): set it up on a volunteer's email or a demo account, including saving backup codes.
- **Pause and verify** (10 min): in pairs, one reads a scam scenario card and the other practices pause-and-verify out loud. Swap. Introduce the family code word ([Agree on a family code word for emergencies](../practices/scams-and-social-engineering/scam-family-code-word.md)).

## Check for understanding (5 min)

1. A text says your bank account is locked and gives a link. What do you do?
2. Your child says someone online is threatening to share a photo unless they pay. What are your first three steps?

Everyone does one item from the [home checklist](../printables/home-spark-checklist.md) before leaving.

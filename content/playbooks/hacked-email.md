**Signs:** password suddenly wrong, friends getting strange messages from you, unfamiliar "new sign-in" alerts, or forwarding rules you didn't make.

## Right now (first 30 minutes)

1. **Use a device you trust.**
2. **Try to log in and change the password.** If you can't, use the official recovery flow: accounts.google.com/signin/recovery (Google), account.live.com/acsr (Microsoft), iforgot.apple.com, or the platform's "hacked account" page such as facebook.com/hacked or instagram.com/hacked. Never use "recovery services" from ads or unsolicited contacts.
3. **Sign out every other session** (security settings, "sign out of all devices").
4. **Turn on two-step login**, or reset it if the attacker changed it. See [Turn on two-step login, starting with your email](../practices/accounts-and-identity/acct-two-step-login.md).

## Close the back doors (same day)

Attackers often add ways back in. Remove anything you don't recognize:

- **Recovery email and phone** ([Make sure you can get back into your own accounts](../practices/accounts-and-identity/acct-recovery-check.md)).
- **Forwarding rules and filters:** in Gmail, Settings > Forwarding and Filters. In Outlook, Rules and Forwarding.
- **Connected apps and devices.**
- **Passkeys and security keys.**
- **Email signature and auto-reply.**

## Contain the damage

- **Change passwords on every account that resets through this email**, banking first ([Let a password manager remember for you](../practices/accounts-and-identity/acct-password-manager.md)).
- **Warn your contacts** from a clean account or by phone: "My account was hacked. Ignore any request for money or links from me between [dates]."
- **Business email:** tell IT or your provider immediately. Search for invoices or payment-change emails sent from the account ([Never change payment details on the strength of an email](../practices/scams-and-social-engineering/scam-payment-change-verification.md)), and consider whether customers must be notified. Call your cyber insurer.
- **If money was involved:** see [Playbook: I think I got scammed](scam-victim.md).

## Afterwards

Scan and update your devices. Report at **ReportFraud.ftc.gov**, or **IdentityTheft.gov** if your identity was misused.

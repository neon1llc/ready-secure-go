---
title: "Playbook: my phone or laptop is lost or stolen"
description: "Locate, lock or erase the device, protect your accounts and phone number, and get back up and running."
tags: [playbook]
---

## Right now

1. **Locate and lock it** from another device: icloud.com/find (Apple), google.com/android/find (Android), or account.microsoft.com/devices (Windows). Mark it lost and show a contact number on the screen.
2. **If it's clearly stolen**, or holds sensitive data you can't recover: **erase it remotely.** An erased Apple device that's activation-locked can still be located.
3. **Call your carrier** to suspend the line and block the SIM, so nobody receives your text-message codes. Ask for a port-out lock or PIN.

## Protect your accounts

4. **Change your email password** first, then banking, from a trusted device, and sign out the lost device on each account's security page.
5. **Payment apps and wallets:** remove the device from Apple Pay, Google Wallet, banking apps, and payment apps.
6. **Work device or work accounts on it?** Tell IT right away so they can wipe it and revoke access. Many organizations must log this.

## Report

- **Theft:** file a police report, which insurance and carrier claims may need. Give them the serial number or IMEI, often in your Apple or Google account.
- Don't confront anyone at an address shown on a map. Give it to the police.

## Get back to normal

- Restore your new device from backup ([Back up what you can't lose, the 3-2-1 way](../practices/data-and-backups/data-3-2-1-backup.md)).
- Set up authenticator apps and passkeys again. Saved backup codes help.

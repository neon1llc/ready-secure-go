title: Playbooks
description: "Step-by-step help when something has already gone wrong."
---

Something happened. Take a breath. These are written to be followed in order, under stress, by anyone.

**First, for any of them:** if money is moving or a child is in danger, act on that first (call the bank, or call 911). Everything else can wait ten minutes.

| What happened | Playbook |
|---|---|
| Someone got into my email or social media | [Playbook: my email or social media account was hacked](hacked-email.md) |
| I think I got scammed, or sent money to a scammer | [Playbook: I think I got scammed](scam-victim.md) |
| My phone or laptop is lost or stolen | [Playbook: my phone or laptop is lost or stolen](lost-or-stolen-phone.md) |
| Our files are locked and there's a ransom note | [Playbook: ransomware in a small office](ransomware-small-office.md) |
| My child is being threatened, blackmailed or harassed online | [Playbook: my child is in trouble online](kid-in-trouble-online.md) |
| I got a letter or email saying my data was in a breach | [Playbook: I got a data breach notice](data-breach-notice.md) |

**Report lines (U.S.):** fraud at **ReportFraud.ftc.gov**, identity theft at **IdentityTheft.gov**, internet crime at **ic3.gov**, child exploitation at **report.cybertip.org**, and older adults at the **National Elder Fraud Hotline, 1-833-FRAUD-11 (1-833-372-8311)**.

Next time, you'll want a [contact sheet](../practices/respond-and-recover/resp-incident-contact-sheet.md) ready before anything happens.

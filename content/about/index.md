## Why this exists

neon1 started at a grade school.

I was working as an after-school aide, and I ended up helping budget and set up a whole new STEM edtech system for the school. Along the way I kept having the same conversation with teachers and parents: *how do I actually keep my kids, my classroom, my own accounts safe?* So I started inviting them to free security debriefings. No slides full of acronyms. Just what's really out there, why it works on people, and what to do about it.

Teaching people live, and watching them leave a little more confident than they came in, is what gave me the confidence to offer the same help more widely. That became neon1. Since then I've run sessions for teachers, parents, engineers, executives and cross-team groups, including seminars in Manhattan for groups of 30 or more. I've also done SOC 2 and other security readiness work for companies.

This collection is those sessions written down, opened up and made free for anyone.

## What I believe about security

- **Security is for everyone, not just security people.** A grandparent who spots a scam call is doing security. So is a teacher who turns on two-step login.
- **Understanding beats memorizing.** If you know *why* something works, you'll recognize the next trick even when it looks different.
- **Start small, then keep going.** Five habits stop most real-world harm. You don't need to be perfect. You just need to not be the easiest target.
- **Look at the whole picture.** When I work with a client, I always look at everything: accounts, devices, networks, cameras, the front door, the people, the vendors. Attackers don't care which department owns a problem.
- **No fear selling.** Honest risk, honest odds, honest fixes.

## The name

The name comes from two characters. Neo, "the One" in *The Matrix*, is an ordinary person who chooses to help other people see clearly. Andrew, the robot in *Bicentennial Man*, spends two hundred years being of service and never stops being curious.

Neo also means new, and neon is hard to miss. The 1 is whoever I'm working with. That's you.

*1 is glad to be of service.*

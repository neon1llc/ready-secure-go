Your network is the road everything travels on. At home it's usually one box in a closet nobody has looked at since the installer left. In an office it's often a tangle of old decisions. A little attention here protects every device behind it.

## Ask yourself

- When did you last log into your router? Do you know how?
- Do guests and smart gadgets share a network with your work computers?
- What can someone on the internet reach in your office right now?

## Practices

<!-- cards:start -->
### 1 · Spark

- [Set up your router like you mean it](../../practices/networks-and-wifi/net-router-basics.md): New admin password, strong Wi-Fi encryption, current firmware, and the risky convenience features turned off.
- [Use public Wi-Fi without worrying about it](../../practices/networks-and-wifi/net-public-wifi.md): Modern encryption makes most public Wi-Fi fine for everyday browsing. Know the few real risks, and when to use your phone's hotspot.

### 2 · Glow

- [Close the doors you left open to the internet](../../practices/networks-and-wifi/net-business-remote-access.md): Find what your office exposes to the internet, put remote access behind MFA, and never expose remote desktop or camera recorders directly.
- [Give guests and gadgets their own Wi-Fi](../../practices/networks-and-wifi/net-guest-network.md): A separate network for visitors and smart devices keeps them away from your computers and files.
- [Secure your cameras and smart devices](../../practices/devices-and-updates/dev-iot-and-cameras.md): Change default passwords, update firmware, turn off what you don't use, and keep cameras and smart gadgets on their own network.
- [Travel light, travel locked](../../practices/physical-and-travel/phys-travel-kit.md): Before a trip, back up, update and lock down your devices, take only what you need, and plan for loss.

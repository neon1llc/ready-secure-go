**Security readiness for every space.**

Most security advice falls into two camps. Either it's written for experts, or it's ten tips that never say *why*. This is neither. It's how I'd explain things if you and I sat down for coffee: plain words, the real reasons, and enough depth that you could explain it to someone else afterward.

## Where are you?

Pick the one that sounds most like you. You can always wander. Or take the five-minute [readiness check](check/index.md) and get your level and next three steps.

- [Me](spaces/me/index.md): just you, your phone, your accounts
- [Home & Family](spaces/home-and-family/index.md): the household, the kids, the router in the closet
- [Seniors & Caregivers](spaces/seniors-and-caregivers/index.md): older adults and the people who look out for them
- [Classroom & School](spaces/classroom-and-school/index.md): teachers, staff, students
- [Home Office](spaces/home-office/index.md): remote work, freelancing, the side business
- [Small Business](spaces/small-business/index.md): offices and shops, 1 to 50 people
- [Nonprofit & Community](spaces/nonprofit-and-community/index.md): doing a lot with very little
- [Growing Organization](spaces/growing-organization/index.md): an IT person, a few systems, maybe an audit coming
- [Public & On the Go](spaces/public-and-on-the-go/index.md): travel, public Wi-Fi, events
- [Builders](spaces/builders/index.md): developers, cloud and IT

**Something already went wrong?** Skip everything else and go to the [playbooks](playbooks/index.md).

## How this works

Every practice here sits in three places at once:

1. A **Space**: who and where you are.
2. A **Light**: which part of security it's about. There are ten, from [Accounts & Identity](lights/accounts-and-identity/index.md) to [Govern & Build](lights/govern-and-build/index.md).
3. A **Level**: how far along you are, from **0 Dark** (not started) to **4 Neon** (you could teach it). See [Levels](levels/index.md).

Start with the **Spark** (level 1) cards in your space. Each one takes an afternoon or less, and together they stop most of what actually happens to real people and small organizations.

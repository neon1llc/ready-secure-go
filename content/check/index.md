The readiness check asks about the practices that fit your Space, one area at a time. Mark each Done, Started, Not yet, or Doesn't fit. You get your [level](../levels/index.md), a grid of strong and thin areas, and the three practices that will help most next.

<p><a class="check-start" href="../static/readiness/">Start the readiness check</a></p>

Your answers stay in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and "Start over" clears them.

> [!question] Ask yourself
> - Would you answer the same way for your home and your office?
> - Who else should take this? A parent, a partner, the person who runs the office?

## Running it with a group

Use the check at the start of a session. Give everyone five minutes, then ask which area came up thinnest for most people. That's your topic. The [teach kits](../teach/index.md) cover the common ones, and the [maturity grid](../levels/grid.md) shows every practice at once.

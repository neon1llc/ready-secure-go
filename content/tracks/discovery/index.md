Whenever I start working with a client, even on something small, I look at their *entire* security setup: accounts, devices, networks, cameras, the front door, the people, the vendors. Problems hide in the gaps between "IT stuff" and "office stuff". This track is that walk-through, so you can run it yourself.

It works for a household, a classroom, a nonprofit or a company. Skip the questions that don't apply.

## How to run it

1. **Set aside two hours** (a home) to two days (an office of 20 to 50).
2. **Walk it, don't just answer it.** Look at the router, the closet, the front desk, the recycling bin, the admin console.
3. **Write answers honestly.** "I don't know" is a finding, and often the most useful one.
4. **Mark each answer:** ✅ fine, ⚠️ needs work, ❌ urgent.
5. **Fix the ❌ first**, then pick the top three ⚠️. Each section links to the practices that help.

## The questions

### Accounts & Identity
- Which accounts matter most (email, bank, payroll, domain, cloud, social)? Who holds each key?
- Is two-step login on all of them? Which kind?
- Are passwords unique, kept in a password manager? Any shared logins?
- Who has admin rights, where? Does anyone use admin accounts day to day?
- Where do account recoveries go? Any personal email addresses on company accounts?

→ [Accounts & Identity](../../lights/accounts-and-identity/index.md)

### Devices & Updates
- List every device: computers, phones, tablets, printers, cameras, recorders, smart devices, point-of-sale terminals.
- Which are out of support? Which haven't updated in a month?
- Are laptops and phones encrypted, with screen locks? Can they be wiped remotely?
- What happens to devices when they're replaced?

→ [Devices & Updates](../../lights/devices-and-updates/index.md)

### Data & Backups
- What data would hurt most to lose or leak? Where does it live?
- Is it backed up 3-2-1? Is one copy safe from ransomware? When was a restore last tested?
- Who can see shared folders? Any "anyone with the link" files with sensitive data?

→ [Data & Backups](../../lights/data-and-backups/index.md)

### Networks & Wi-Fi
- Who manages the router or firewall? Is the admin password changed, firmware current?
- Are guests, smart devices, staff and payment systems on separate networks?
- What's reachable from the internet (remote desktop, VPN, camera recorder, file shares)?
- How do people work remotely?

→ [Networks & Wi-Fi](../../lights/networks-and-wifi/index.md)

### Cameras, monitoring and smart devices
- Where are cameras pointed? Who can view them, and from where? Any old users with access?
- Are default passwords changed? Is the recorder exposed to the internet?
- Where are recordings stored, and for how long? Who can export them?

→ [Secure your cameras and smart devices](../../practices/devices-and-updates/dev-iot-and-cameras.md)

### Scams & Social Engineering
- Has anyone received fake invoices, gift card requests, or "CEO" messages? What happened?
- Is there a payment-change verification rule? Does everyone know it?
- How do people report suspicious messages?

→ [Scams & Social Engineering](../../lights/scams-and-social-engineering/index.md)

### Privacy, Footprint and the Dark Web
- What does a search of the owner's or key staff's names show? Are home addresses on people-search sites?
- Have company email addresses appeared in breaches? Were those passwords changed?
- What does the organization's website or social media reveal (staff lists, org charts, email formats, photos of badges or screens)?

→ [Privacy & Footprint](../../lights/privacy-and-footprint/index.md)

### People & Culture
- What security training happens, and when? Would people report a mistake?
- Is there an onboarding and offboarding checklist? Test it: pick the last person who left and check their access.
- For families: have the kids and grandparents had the tech talk?

→ [People & Culture](../../lights/people-and-culture/index.md)

### Physical & Travel
- How do people get in? Keys, codes, badges, passcards? Who has them? Are lost ones turned off?
- Is there a visitor process? Can someone tailgate?
- Where is the network equipment? Locked? Are wall ports live in public areas?
- Screens visible from windows or reception? Paper records secured and shredded?

→ [Physical & Travel](../../lights/physical-and-travel/index.md)

### Respond & Recover
- Is there a contact sheet on paper? Does it include insurance, IT, bank and lawyer?
- Has anyone ever practiced an incident?
- Do you have cyber insurance? What does it require of you?

→ [Respond & Recover](../../lights/respond-and-recover/index.md)

### Govern & Build
- Is there an inventory of systems and vendors with owners?
- Have important vendors been reviewed? Who can reach your systems remotely?
- Are there written policies, and do they match reality?
- For builders: how do code and infrastructure changes reach production? Where do secrets live?
- Do customers or regulators expect something specific (SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI DSS, FERPA)?

→ [Govern & Build](../../lights/govern-and-build/index.md)

## After discovery

Turn findings into a short plan: **this week** (the ❌ items), **this month** (top three ⚠️), **this quarter** (the rest worth doing). Then place yourself on the [levels](../../levels/index.md) for each Light, and do it again in a year.

With every client, even on something small, I look at their *entire* security setup: accounts, devices, networks, cameras, the front door, the people, and the vendors. Problems hide in the gaps between "IT stuff" and "office stuff". It works for a household, a classroom, a nonprofit, or a company. Skip questions that don't apply.

## How to run it

1. **Set aside two hours** for a home or two days for an office of 20 to 50.
2. **Walk it.** Look at the router, the closet, the front desk, the recycling bin, the admin console.
3. **Answer honestly.** "I don't know" is a finding.
4. **Mark each answer:** ✅ fine, ⚠️ needs work, ❌ urgent.
5. **Fix the ❌ first**, then the top three ⚠️. Each heading links to practices that help.

## The questions

### [Accounts & Identity](../../lights/accounts-and-identity/index.md)
- Which accounts matter most (email, bank, payroll, domain, cloud, social), and who holds each key?
- Is two-step login on all of them? Are passwords unique, in a password manager, and unshared?
- Who has admin rights, and does anyone use an admin account day to day?
- Where do recoveries go? Any personal email addresses on company accounts?

### [Devices & Updates](../../lights/devices-and-updates/index.md)
- List every device: computers, phones, tablets, printers, cameras, recorders, smart devices, point-of-sale terminals.
- Which are out of support, or haven't updated in a month?
- Are laptops and phones encrypted, locked, and remotely wipeable?
- What happens to a device when it's replaced?
- Cameras: who can view them, do old users still have access, and are default passwords changed? ([Secure your cameras and smart devices](../../practices/devices-and-updates/dev-iot-and-cameras.md))

### [Data & Backups](../../lights/data-and-backups/index.md)
- What data would hurt most to lose or leak, and where does it live?
- Is it backed up 3-2-1, with one copy safe from ransomware? When was a restore last tested?
- Are any sensitive files shared with "anyone with the link"?

### [Networks & Wi-Fi](../../lights/networks-and-wifi/index.md)
- Is the router or firewall admin password changed and the firmware current?
- Are guests, smart devices, staff, and payment systems on separate networks?
- What's reachable from the internet (remote desktop, VPN, camera recorder, file shares)?

### [Scams & Social Engineering](../../lights/scams-and-social-engineering/index.md)
- Has anyone received fake invoices, gift card requests, or "CEO" messages? What happened?
- Does everyone know the payment-change rule, and how to report suspicious messages?

### [Privacy & Footprint](../../lights/privacy-and-footprint/index.md)
- Are the owner's or key staff's home addresses on people-search sites?
- Have company email addresses appeared in breaches, and were those passwords changed?
- Does your website or social media reveal staff lists, email formats, or photos of badges or screens?

### [People & Culture](../../lights/people-and-culture/index.md)
- Would people report a mistake? What security training happens?
- Is there an onboarding and offboarding checklist? Test it on the last person who left.
- For families: have the kids and grandparents had the tech talk?

### [Physical & Travel](../../lights/physical-and-travel/index.md)
- How do people get in (keys, codes, badges)? Are lost ones turned off? Can someone tailgate?
- Is the network equipment locked away? Are wall ports live in public areas?
- Can screens be seen from windows or reception? Are paper records secured and shredded?

### [Respond & Recover](../../lights/respond-and-recover/index.md)
- Is there a paper contact sheet with insurance, IT, bank, and lawyer? Has anyone practiced an incident?
- Do you have cyber insurance, and what does it require of you?

### [Govern & Build](../../lights/govern-and-build/index.md)
- Is there an inventory of systems and vendors with owners? Have important vendors been reviewed?
- Do written policies match reality?
- For builders: how do changes reach production, and where do secrets live?
- Do customers or regulators expect something specific (SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI DSS, FERPA)?

## After discovery

Make a plan: **this week** for the ❌ items, **this month** for the top three ⚠️, **this quarter** for the rest worth doing. Place yourself on the [levels](../../levels/index.md) for each Light, and repeat in a year.

**Our household is Spark-ready when:**

- [ ] Everyone's main email has **two-step login** on, and backup codes are saved. ([Turn on two-step login, starting with your email](../practices/accounts-and-identity/acct-two-step-login.md))
- [ ] We use a **password manager**, and email, bank and phone carrier have unique passwords. ([Let a password manager remember for you](../practices/accounts-and-identity/acct-password-manager.md))
- [ ] **Automatic updates** are on for phones, computers, tablets and the router. ([Turn on automatic updates, everywhere](../practices/devices-and-updates/dev-auto-updates.md))
- [ ] Phones and laptops have a **passcode** and **Find My** is on. ([Set up find-my-device and remote wipe before you need it](../practices/devices-and-updates/dev-find-and-wipe.md))
- [ ] Photos and important files are **backed up**, with one copy off-site. ([Back up what you can't lose, the 3-2-1 way](../practices/data-and-backups/data-3-2-1-backup.md))
- [ ] The **router** admin password has been changed, and Wi-Fi uses WPA2 or WPA3. ([Set up your router like you mean it](../practices/networks-and-wifi/net-router-basics.md))
- [ ] We know the **scam reflex**: pause, hang up, call back on a number we already have. ([Learn the scam reflex, pause and verify](../practices/scams-and-social-engineering/scam-pause-and-verify.md))
- [ ] We have a **family code word** for emergencies. ([Agree on a family code word for emergencies](../practices/scams-and-social-engineering/scam-family-code-word.md))
- [ ] **Credit is frozen** for the adults, and for the kids too. ([Freeze your credit (and your kids' credit)](../practices/privacy-and-footprint/priv-credit-freeze.md))
- [ ] We've had the **family tech talk**, and the kids know they can tell us anything. ([Have the family tech talk, and keep having it](../practices/people-and-culture/ppl-family-tech-talk.md))

**If something goes wrong:** bank fraud line (back of card) · ReportFraud.ftc.gov · IdentityTheft.gov · Elder Fraud Hotline 1-833-372-8311 · report.cybertip.org

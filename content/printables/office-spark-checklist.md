**Our office is Spark-ready when:**

- [ ] **MFA** is required for email, remote access, banking and every admin account. ([Turn on two-step login, starting with your email](../practices/accounts-and-identity/acct-two-step-login.md))
- [ ] **Money rule:** payment-detail changes and unusual payment requests are verified by calling a known number. Nobody buys gift cards because of an email. ([Never change payment details on the strength of an email](../practices/scams-and-social-engineering/scam-payment-change-verification.md))
- [ ] **Backups** run automatically, one copy can't be reached by ransomware, and we've tested a restore. ([Back up what you can't lose, the 3-2-1 way](../practices/data-and-backups/data-3-2-1-backup.md))
- [ ] **Updates** are automatic on every computer, phone, router and firewall. ([Turn on automatic updates, everywhere](../practices/devices-and-updates/dev-auto-updates.md))
- [ ] **Everyday accounts aren't admins.** Admin accounts are separate and few. ([Keep admin power out of everyday accounts](../practices/accounts-and-identity/acct-admin-separation.md))
- [ ] **Leavers lose access** on their last day, with a checklist. ([Give access on day one and take it all back on the last day](../practices/people-and-culture/ppl-onboarding-offboarding.md))
- [ ] **One way to report** a suspicious message, and no blame for reporting, even "I clicked". ([Make reporting a phish easy, quick and blame-free](../practices/scams-and-social-engineering/scam-report-phishing.md))
- [ ] **Laptops** are encrypted and lock themselves. ([Lock your screens and encrypt your devices](../practices/devices-and-updates/dev-screen-lock-and-encryption.md))
- [ ] **Guests** use a separate Wi-Fi network. ([Give guests and gadgets their own Wi-Fi](../practices/networks-and-wifi/net-guest-network.md))
- [ ] **The "bad day" sheet** is printed: IT, insurer, bank, lawyer, and the first three steps. ([Write your "if something goes wrong" sheet, on paper](../practices/respond-and-recover/resp-incident-contact-sheet.md))

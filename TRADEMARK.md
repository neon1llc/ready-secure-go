# Trademark and name use

"neon1", the neon1 wordmark and the neon1 logo belong to neon1 LLC. The open licenses in this repository cover the words, checklists and code. They don't cover the name or logo.

**You're welcome to:**

- Say that your work is based on or adapted from Ready, Secure, Go, with a link back. That's the whole point.
- Print and hand out unmodified pages, logo included, for workshops, classrooms, community groups and offices.
- Mention neon1 when you review it, teach from it or write about it.

**Please don't:**

- Use the name or logo for a product, service, course or site in a way that suggests neon1 made it or endorses it.
- Hand out modified material under the neon1 name. If you change it, remove the logo and say it's adapted.

Not sure? Ask. The answer is usually yes.
